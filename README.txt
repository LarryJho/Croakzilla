================================================================
 THE HUNT OF CROAKZILLA
 by LarryJho
================================================================

You are Edward Salami. The local national park has called you in
to deal with a giant mutant animal that's destroying the ecosystem,
and a rat plague spreading through the woods.

Earn the highest score you can — a 5-star recommendation means
more contracts. Don't kill the snakes; they're a protected species
and you LOSE points for each one.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 RUN SETUP
----------------------------------------------------------------

Each run, after pressing START on the main menu, you'll pick:

  1. DIFFICULTY  : Baby / Normal / Hell / This Doesn't Make Any Sense
  2. BEAM MODE   : Fixed (cardinal aim) / Cursor (mouse aim)

Both choices persist for the run; restart from the main menu to
change them.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 CONTROLS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  MOVE         WASD  or  Arrow keys
  SHOOT        Space (any mode)
               Left-click (CURSOR beam mode only)
  PUNCH        Left-click on an enemy near you  (FIXED mode only)
  WALK TO      Left-click on the floor          (FIXED mode only)
  ZOOM         Mouse wheel up / down
  PAUSE        ESC  (also toggles the pause menu)

Beam mode details:

  FIXED  : beam fires in the direction you last moved (4 cardinals).
           Left-click punches adjacent enemies or walks to the spot.
  CURSOR : beam fires toward the mouse cursor (any angle). The
           sprite turns to face the cursor for each shot. Movement
           is keyboard-only — left-click always fires.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 STATS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  HEALTH       Red bar. At 0 you die.
  HUNGER       Blue bar. At 0 you start taking damage.
  ENERGY       Yellow bar. Recharges automatically; needed to fire
               the gun you stole from a government facility.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 ENEMIES 
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Big Rat        +5     Skittish. Runs but turns to fight when cornered.
  Snake          -10    PROTECTED. Do not engage.
  Giant Shroom   +1     Don't wake it up. Persistent once roused.


----------------------------------------------------------------
 PICKUPS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Heart          +10 HP        (drops from any kill, 8% chance)
  Battery        +5 energy     (drops from any kill, 15% chance —
                                doubled to 30% on Hell / Insane to
                                keep the ammo economy sustainable)
  Apple          +20 hunger    (10% chance per room)
  Prize          Permanent buff. Stacks up to 2 per type.
                 Normal pool : range, damage, radius, speed, shield
                 Elite pool  : same effects scaled up, with damage
                               bonus baked in (dark aura).

  Shield reduces each incoming enemy hit by N damage (N = total
  shield stacks). Hits always do at least 1 damage even with full
  shield, so you can't be invincible.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 DIFFICULTY
----------------------------------------------------------------

  BABY                    Easy. Half damage taken (EXCEPT from
                          Croakzilla), slower enemies, half the
                          rooms and enemies, +20 HP heal on boss-
                          room entry, hunger doesn't kill,
                          Croakzilla 0.6x speed.
  NORMAL                  Default balanced run.
  HELL                    2x rooms + 2x enemies, slower energy
                          recharge, x2 damage in boss rooms,
                          2x battery drop chance.
  THIS DOESN'T MAKE       2x rooms + 3x enemies (same map size
   ANY SENSE              as Hell, but denser), x2 damage taken
                          always, faster player AND enemies,
                          2x battery drop chance.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 STAR RATING (You Win screen)
----------------------------------------------------------------

                       1*    2*    3*    4*    5*
  Baby              ≤88    ≤125  ≤163  ≤200  >200
  Normal            ≤175   ≤250  ≤325  ≤400  >400
  Hell / Insane     ≤200   ≤280  ≤360  ≤440  >440

----------------------------------------------------------------
 TIPS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - Snakes give negative score. Just walk past them.
  - Hearts, Batteries and Apples spawn often — search every room.
  - Stack shields early: every shield point trims 1 damage from
    every enemy hit. Two normal stacks + two elite stacks = 6 shield.
  - The Marshlands boss room has a one-way trip. Heal up and stock
    energy before entering.

----------------------------------------------------------------

Have fun, and don't get eaten.
